# Zevora Styles — Policy Pack

## Clause 3.1 — Size Exchange
Wrong size reported within 7 days of delivery → one free exchange. Customer must return item in original condition with tags.

## Clause 4.2 — Damaged on Arrival
Visible tear, stain, or manufacturing defect at delivery → full refund or replacement within 48 hours of delivery. Evidence required: photo or video of damage with price tag visible in the same frame.

## Clause 5.1 — Delivery Delay
Order not delivered within 10 days of promised delivery date → customer may request full refund.

## Clause 6.0 — Auto-Resolution Limit
Disputes under the seller's configured auto-resolve limit may be approved automatically without seller review.

## Clause 7.3 — Fraud
AI-generated, manipulated, or misrepresented evidence → dispute rejected and account flagged for review.
